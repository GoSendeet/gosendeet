## The quick answer

There is no single delivery price for Nigeria. Your pickup and delivery addresses, packed weight, parcel size and selected service determine the quote. Compare current prices for the same parcel and route before paying.

## Why can the same parcel cost more this time?

A quote from a previous order is a budgeting reference, not today’s price. Fuel costs, operating expenses and the service selected can affect shipping charges. DHL’s Nigerian shipping guidance identifies fuel and packaging choices among the factors affecting costs.

For a Lagos seller sending a small order to Ibadan, the useful question is: what will this exact packed order cost to this exact address today? A city name alone is not enough to compare doorstep and collection services.

## What should I have ready for a delivery quote?

Record the complete pickup and destination addresses, working phone numbers, item description, packed weight and dimensions. Include a landmark where an address is difficult to locate.

Weigh the parcel after packing. A light item in a large box may attract a size-based charge, depending on the courier. Ask how the provider calculates chargeable weight rather than assuming all providers use the same formula.

## How do I compare courier prices fairly?

Use identical addresses, parcel details and pickup requirements for every quote. Check whether the price covers pickup and delivery to the recipient’s door, or requires someone to visit a collection point.

Before payment, check the estimated delivery window, item restrictions, cancellation conditions and any protection offered for loss or damage. A lower headline price can be a poor fit if it excludes a service you need.

## How can I reduce delivery costs without making promises I cannot keep?

Use a snug, protective package and confirm the recipient’s address and availability before booking. If an order is not urgent, compare a standard service with an express option.

Online sellers can show the delivery charge separately and confirm it before accepting an order. If you advertise free delivery, build the actual delivery expense into your margin calculation. Gosendeet’s quote calculator is a starting point for checking available options; availability depends on your route.

## Common questions

### Is there a fixed delivery fee from Lagos to Ibadan?

No universal fee applies. Get a current quote using the actual addresses and packed parcel details.

### Is the cheapest courier always the best option?

Choose a quote that meets your pickup, delivery timing and item requirements. Compare the total charge and service conditions, not only price.
