## The quick answer

Check the latest tracking update and the delivery window on your booking. If that window has passed, contact the provider with your booking reference, last update and confirmed recipient details. Ask for a specific next step and revised estimate.

## Does a tracking gap mean my parcel is lost?

A missing update alone does not establish that a parcel is lost. Ask the courier to confirm its last recorded location and whether pickup or delivery has actually taken place.

Save a screenshot of the status and the time you checked it. Separate what tracking confirms from what you suspect, especially when updating a customer.

## What information should I give support?

Send the booking or tracking reference, booking date, pickup and destination areas, expected delivery window and last recorded status. Confirm whether the sender handed over the parcel and whether the recipient received a call.

A useful message is: “Booking [reference] was collected on [date]. The last update is [status]. The expected window has passed. Please confirm the parcel location, the next action and the revised delivery estimate.” Share personal details through the provider’s support channel rather than a public comment.

## What if tracking says delivered but the recipient has nothing?

Ask the recipient to check with authorised reception staff or anyone permitted to accept the parcel. Request proof of delivery from the provider and report the mismatch promptly.

Keep your packaging photos, invoice, booking confirmation and messages. Ask about the provider’s investigation and claim process; eligibility, limits and reporting deadlines depend on the terms attached to your booking.

## How should an online seller update the buyer?

Send the confirmed status, the action you have taken and when you will next update them. Avoid announcing a new arrival time until the provider confirms it.

If a return or replacement is needed, agree the next step with the buyer and check the courier’s return arrangements. On Gosendeet, start with the tracking page and use the site’s support channel for a booking enquiry.

## Common questions

### Can I claim a refund for a late parcel?

Check the service terms and ask the provider. A delay does not automatically qualify for a refund under every delivery service.

### Should I book another delivery straight away?

First confirm what happened to the original parcel. Sending a replacement before an investigation can create duplicate deliveries and extra costs.
