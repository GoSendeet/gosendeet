## The quick answer

To send a parcel from Lagos to Ibadan, confirm your item is accepted, enter both full addresses, compare available quotes and prepare the parcel for pickup. Confirm the estimated delivery window before promising an arrival date.

## Can I get same-day delivery from Lagos to Ibadan?

Ask for availability for your exact route and booking time. Do not assume that a same-day service advertised for deliveries within Lagos also applies to an interstate parcel.

Check whether the estimate starts from booking, actual pickup or receipt at a courier facility. If the parcel must arrive for an event, tell the provider the deadline before paying and ask what happens if pickup is delayed.

## Will the parcel go to a doorstep or a collection point?

Confirm the handover method at both ends. Doorstep delivery, depot collection and pickup from your address are different services. A quote is only useful if everyone understands what it includes.

GIG Logistics’ FAQ makes home delivery conditional on listed delivery locations. That illustrates why you should verify address-level coverage with whichever courier you choose.

## What details prevent avoidable delivery problems?

Give the street address, area, city, recipient’s name and reachable phone number. Add a helpful landmark and access instructions for an estate, office or shop. Ask the recipient to confirm these details before booking.

Pack the item securely, seal the parcel and photograph its condition. Keep the booking confirmation and tracking reference. For fragile or valuable items, ask about acceptance, handling and claim conditions in advance.

## What should I do after pickup?

Share the tracking reference with your recipient and check the recorded progress. If the estimated window passes, contact support with the booking reference, last recorded update and recipient details.

Use Gosendeet to check available quotes and its tracking page for bookings made through the platform. A service appearing for one route does not establish coverage for every address in Lagos or Ibadan.

## Common questions

### Can I send food or a phone to Ibadan?

Acceptance depends on the item and courier. Describe the contents accurately, especially food, liquids or items containing batteries, and confirm restrictions before booking.

### What if the receiver is unavailable?

Contact the provider promptly to ask about another attempt or collection. Redelivery availability and charges depend on the service conditions.
