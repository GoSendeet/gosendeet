# Shipping from Lagos to Ibadan: How Much Does It Cost, and How Long Does It Take?

By Damilare Gosendeet

Your customer in Ibadan has paid. The parcel is ready in Lagos, and they want to know two things: how much is delivery, and when will it arrive? Guessing an old waybill price or promising “tomorrow” before checking the service can create avoidable trouble.

**Get a current quote for both full addresses and the packed parcel, then confirm the estimated delivery window and handover method.** Lagos to Ibadan does not have one universal parcel price or arrival time.

## Choose the delivery method before comparing prices

A terminal service and a doorstep service solve different problems. With terminal collection, someone usually has to receive the parcel at the named collection point. Doorstep delivery may be more convenient, but you need to confirm that the destination address is served.

[GIG Logistics' FAQ](https://giglogistics.com/mfaq/) makes home delivery conditional on listed locations. This is a useful reminder to check address-level coverage with whichever provider you select.

For a customer who cannot leave work, a cheaper terminal quote might be unsuitable. For someone living close to the collection point, it could be practical.

## What information determines the quote?

Supply the contents, packed weight and dimensions, pickup location and destination address. Tell the provider if the parcel is fragile, unusually valuable or needed before a fixed event.

Include the recipient's name, phone number and a helpful landmark. “Ibadan” or “Ring Road” alone may not tell a courier where to hand over the parcel.

Ask whether the total includes collection in Lagos, interstate transport and the final delivery leg in Ibadan. If you are comparing a waybill service, check terminal opening hours and collection requirements too.

## When does the delivery clock start?

Clarify whether the estimate starts when you book, when the courier collects or when the parcel reaches a facility. A booking placed late in the day may not enter transport immediately.

Ask how weekends, public holidays and missed collection affect the estimate. Do not use the driving time between the cities as the parcel delivery promise: sorting, scheduling and final handover are part of the journey.

You can ask:

> This parcel is ready at [pickup address]. It needs to reach [destination address] before [deadline]. What is the earliest available collection, the estimated arrival window and the total charge?

## Prepare the parcel and recipient

Seal the package securely and label it according to the provider's instructions. Photograph its condition and keep your booking record. Confirm that someone can receive it or collect it from the agreed terminal.

Describe contents accurately. Food, liquids and electronics can require different acceptance checks; the fact that an item is going by road does not remove courier restrictions.

## Can I get same-day delivery?

Only promise it after confirming a supported service and its conditions for that booking. An intracity same-day offer does not establish same-day interstate delivery.
