# How Do I Deliver Clothes to Customers Across Nigeria?

By Seun Gosendeet

Someone in another state wants to buy from your clothing business. That is encouraging, but “we deliver nationwide” becomes a real responsibility when you must quote the order, send the correct items and help the customer receive them.

**Build your delivery offer around confirmed routes, clear handover methods and correctly packed orders.** Serving customers in different states does not require one provider or one fee, but each order needs an arrangement you understand.

## Confirm coverage before taking an arrival commitment

Ask for the full delivery address and a reachable recipient number. Check whether the available service delivers there or requires collection from a terminal.

A courier serving a city does not establish doorstep coverage for every nearby town, estate or rural address. Tell the customer the available method before confirming the order.

If no suitable service is available, offer an alternative only when the customer agrees and you can verify it. Do not advertise a route based on an assumption that someone will find a rider later.

## Build a delivery map from real orders

Record destinations you have served, the method used, quoted delivery windows and any problems. Over time, this gives your shop a practical reference for what to check next.

Keep prices current. A successful delivery last month is evidence that the route worked then, not a permanent promise of the same price or timing.

Separate your preparation time from transit. A made-to-order outfit still in production has not started its delivery journey.

## Give the buyer a complete order summary

Before payment, confirm garments, sizes, colours, quantities, product price, delivery charge and handover method. Explain when you plan to dispatch and the selected service's estimated arrival window.

For an event outfit, ask for the date the buyer actually needs it. If the available timing leaves little room, explain that before accepting the order.

Read [how to charge customers for delivery](08-delivery-fees-for-customers.md) if you are deciding whether to quote individually or subsidise certain orders.

## Pack for the journey and check the label

Check each item against the order record before sealing it. Protect garments from dirt and moisture with suitable inner packing, then use outer packaging accepted by the provider.

Keep shoes or other accessories from rubbing against delicate clothing. Describe the contents accurately and measure the final parcel after packing.

Check that the recipient label matches the garments inside. A wrong-size parcel sent across states creates a second shipping problem, even if the original transport was successful.

## Explain exchanges before dispatch

Give customers clear instructions for reporting a size issue, requesting an exchange and arranging any approved return. Set out the applicable policy and agreed delivery costs before the sale.

Do not promise automatic reverse pickup or free exchange delivery unless you can support it. Check each return route and the product issue before deciding the next action.

For terminal shipments, tell the receiver what they need to collect and when the facility is open.
