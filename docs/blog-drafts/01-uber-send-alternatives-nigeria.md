# I Used Uber Send for Deliveries. What Do I Use Now?

By Damilare Gosendeet

You had a familiar routine: open Uber, arrange a parcel trip, and get back to your day. Now you need another way to send that customer order, forgotten charger or document. Starting again should not mean handing your parcel to the first person who answers a message.

**The practical replacement depends on what you used Uber Send for.** An urgent trip across town, an interstate parcel and daily shop orders need different services. Compare the actual pickup, destination and package rather than choosing an app by name alone.

## What changed?

[Legit.ng reported on 3 September 2026](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/industry/1728741-uber-nigerian-smes-options-6-delivery-services-consider/) that Uber's Nigeria exit also closed Uber Send, affecting businesses that used it to move goods. This guide addresses parcel delivery, so a passenger ride-hailing alternative is not automatically a suitable replacement.

## Match the replacement to the job

| Your situation | What to look for |
|---|---|
| A small parcel needs to cross town | Available pickup, a suitable vehicle and a realistic arrival estimate |
| A parcel is going to another state | A courier serving both addresses and a clear handover method |
| You send customer orders regularly | Consistent booking records, tracking and a reachable support channel |
| You are sending something valuable | Written acceptance and liability conditions for that item |

[Kwik](https://kwik.delivery/) advertises on-demand logistics, [GIG Logistics](https://giglogistics.com/mfaq/) offers parcel services, and [Sendbox](https://sendbox.co/) describes delivery services for personal and social-commerce use. These are options to investigate, not a tested ranking. Check current availability, charges and conditions directly.

## Ask five questions before paying

1. Can you collect from this exact address at the required time?
2. Does the price include delivery to the recipient, or collection at a terminal?
3. What reference will I receive, and how will I track the parcel?
4. Who handles a missed pickup, an unavailable receiver or a delivery problem?
5. Is the item accepted, and what happens if it is damaged or lost?

Keep the parcel details identical when comparing prices. One quote for a collection-point service and another for doorstep delivery are different offers, even if the route names match.

If a provider wants to move the booking outside its official process, establish which tracking, support and payment protections you would lose before agreeing. Familiarity with an individual rider does not replace a clear delivery record.

## Can I use any ride-hailing app to send a parcel?

Check for a supported parcel product in your city. A car being available for passengers does not establish parcel acceptance, tracking or responsibility for loss.
