# How Much Should a Delivery Cost Within Lagos?

By Seun Gosendeet

You ask how much it will cost to send a parcel within Lagos and receive three different answers. One sounds cheap, another feels expensive, and none explains whether the recipient will actually get the item at their door.

**A reasonable Lagos delivery price is a current quote for your exact route, packed parcel and required service.** There is no reliable single fee for every trip within the city. To judge a quote, compare matching services and check the total cost.

## Why “within Lagos” is not enough

Ikeja to Yaba, Surulere to Lekki and Ajah to Ikorodu are different journeys. A nearby pickup, a long cross-city trip and an address outside a courier's usual service area can require different arrangements.

The item matters too. A document envelope and a bulky box should not be priced as though they create the same transport requirements. [DHL's Nigerian shipping guidance](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-ng/logistics-advice/logistics-insights/what-s-driving-your-shipping-costs-in-nigeria-right-now-) identifies fuel and packaging among the factors affecting shipping costs.

## Give every provider the same information

Prepare one set of details and reuse it:

| Detail | What to record |
|---|---|
| Pickup | Full address and contact person |
| Delivery | Full destination address and receiver's phone number |
| Parcel | Contents, packed weight and dimensions |
| Timing | Earliest collection and latest useful arrival |
| Handover | Doorstep delivery or collection point |

Ask when each quote expires and whether it can change if the actual parcel differs from your description. Weigh the package after packing, not before adding the box and protective material.

## Compare the whole bill

Check whether collection is included. Ask about extra charges for waiting, failed collection, redelivery or an address change. [Gosendeet's terms](https://gosendeet.com/terms) explain that carrier charges may arise from shipment discrepancies and other specified circumstances.

A hypothetical example shows why this matters: one offer is ₦3,500 with pickup included; another is ₦3,000 but requires you to spend ₦1,000 getting to a drop-off point. Your cash expense is ₦3,500 versus ₦4,000. These figures illustrate comparison arithmetic; they are not current Lagos rates.

Your time also matters. A collection-point option may still suit someone who is already passing the terminal, while being inconvenient for someone at work.

## When should I question a high quote?

Ask what service you are paying for. An urgent dedicated trip can be a different product from a flexible next-day parcel service. Request a less urgent option if your deadline allows it.

If comparable quotes remain far apart, check that all providers understood the route, dimensions and delivery method correctly. A very low quote with no clear support process deserves questions too.

## Does a higher price guarantee faster delivery?

No. Check the named service and its delivery estimate. Price alone is not a timing commitment.
