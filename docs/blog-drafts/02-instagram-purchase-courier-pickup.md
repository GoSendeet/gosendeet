# I Bought Something on Instagram. Can a Courier Pick It Up for Me?

By Ore

The seller has the item you want, but their shop is far away or they do not arrange delivery. You have paid, or are ready to pay, and the next question is straightforward: can someone collect it for you?

**Yes, a courier can collect a prepared parcel from a willing seller where the service is available.** Agree the handover with the seller before booking. The courier collects and delivers the parcel. Check with the provider if you also need someone to inspect it or pay the seller.

## First, confirm the order is ready

Ask the seller to confirm the exact item, quantity and any size, colour or model details. If there is a balance to pay, settle how that will be handled before a rider arrives. Do not assume a courier will pay for goods on your behalf.

Request a picture of the packed parcel if possible. It can help document what the seller says is ready, although a photograph does not prove that the contents match your order.

If you have doubts about the seller, resolve them before sending a courier. A pickup booking cannot make an unverified vendor trustworthy.

## Get a complete pickup address

An Instagram handle is not a pickup address. Ask for:

- The shop or contact person's name and working phone number.
- The full address, area and a useful landmark.
- The hours when someone can hand over the order.
- An order reference that identifies your parcel.
- The packed weight, dimensions and accurate contents description.

Use the seller's details for pickup and your details for delivery. A courier needs a reachable person at each end, especially where an estate gate, office reception or market location requires directions.

## Agree who is booking and paying

Either party may arrange transport, depending on the provider. Agree who will book so you do not accidentally pay for two collections.

You can send the seller this message:

> Please confirm order [reference] is packed and ready. I will arrange courier pickup from [address]. Who should the courier call, what hours can they collect, and what are the parcel's packed weight and dimensions?

Then share the booking details with the seller. Ask them to check the collection details against the booking before releasing the parcel and to let you know when the courier collects it.

## What if the courier arrives and the parcel is not ready?

Contact the provider to find out whether the rider can wait. A wasted trip, cancelled collection or second attempt may carry a charge under the selected service's terms. Confirm readiness first to reduce that risk.

## Will the courier open the package and check it for me?

Do not assume so. Inspection, purchase verification and cash handling require an explicit supported arrangement. If you need any of them, ask before payment.

## What should I do if the parcel is wrong?

Keep the order messages, packaging and handover records. Contact the seller about the product discrepancy and the delivery provider about any transport issue. The seller handles problems with the order; the courier handles problems during delivery.
