# What’s the Cheapest Way to Send a Parcel Within Lagos?

By Seun Gosendeet

You need to send a parcel, but it does not need a premium express trip. The challenge is finding a lower-cost service that still collects and delivers in a way that works for both people.

**The cheapest suitable option is the one with the lowest complete cost that meets your parcel, address and timing requirements.** There is no courier that is cheapest for every Lagos route.

## Decide what you can be flexible about

Before searching for a price, identify the non-negotiables. Is the parcel needed today? Must it reach a door, or can the receiver collect? Can you drop it off somewhere without a separate journey?

If the deadline is flexible, ask providers whether a standard or scheduled service is available alongside urgent dispatch. A lower-cost option only helps if its delivery window is acceptable.

Avoid treating an advertised intracity service as automatically available at every address. Use the actual pickup and destination.

## Compare four ways of sending

| Method | Potential advantage | Possible extra expense |
|---|---|---|
| Pickup and doorstep delivery | Less travel for sender and receiver | The quoted collection and delivery charge |
| Drop-off and doorstep delivery | May avoid a paid collection leg | Your trip to the drop-off point |
| Terminal or collection-point service | Can suit people near the facility | The receiver's travel and collection time |
| Supported combined shipment | Can reduce duplicated transport | Larger size, extra handling or additional stops |

These are methods to investigate, not a claim that every provider offers all four. [GIG Logistics' FAQ](https://giglogistics.com/mfaq/) distinguishes location-dependent home-delivery availability, so check the handover conditions of its quote or any other service you choose.

## Pack compactly without removing protection

Choose packaging that fits the item. Excess empty space can make the package larger than necessary, but inadequate protection can turn a small saving into a damaged order.

Keep fragile items protected and confirm acceptance. Do not describe a valuable or restricted item as a harmless small parcel to obtain a cheaper quote.

If several items are going to one receiver, compare one correctly measured package with separate parcels. It is not always cheaper, especially where combining them changes the handling requirement.

## Count your own transport costs

Imagine a ₦2,500 collection-point quote that requires ₦1,500 of additional transport, compared with ₦3,500 for doorstep delivery. The collection-point option costs ₦4,000 in total. These are hypothetical figures used to show the calculation.

Check the receiver's journey too. Saving the sender money by giving the receiver a costly collection trip may not be a good outcome.

## Keep cheap and uncertain separate

Get a clear booking record, reachable support channel and agreed delivery method. An informal price with no traceable handover is difficult to assess against a documented service.

Ask about waiting, failed collection and redelivery charges. A preventable second attempt can outweigh a small difference between initial quotes.
