# My Parcel Hasn’t Arrived. How Do I Track It and Get Help?

By Damilare Gosendeet

The expected delivery window has passed. The recipient has nothing, the tracking page has not answered your question, and you are wondering whether to keep waiting or escalate.

**Start with the booking reference, the latest confirmed status and evidence of collection.** Then contact the booking provider with a clear request for the next action. A delay needs investigation; it does not by itself prove that a parcel is lost.

## Check what was promised

Find the service name and delivery estimate in your confirmation. Check whether it refers to working days, starts at collection and excludes particular dates or locations.

Look at the last update and its timestamp. “Booked” may mean an order was created; it should not be treated as proof that the sender handed over the item.

Ask the sender when collection actually happened. If the seller has only promised to dispatch, the issue may still be at the pickup stage.

## Work out which stage needs attention

| What you know | Useful next question |
|---|---|
| Booking created, no collection confirmed | Was the parcel ready and was collection attempted? |
| Collected, no recent movement | What is the last confirmed location and next planned action? |
| Delivery attempted | Who was contacted, and what options are available now? |
| Marked delivered, recipient has nothing | Can the provider supply the handover details or proof of delivery? |

Status wording differs across providers. Ask for an explanation of the actual status rather than guessing what a label means.

## Send a message support can act on

Include the reference, collection date, expected window, last status and destination area. Explain whether the receiver received a call or whether access was difficult.

You can use:

> Booking [reference] was collected on [date]. The expected window was [window]. Tracking last showed [status] at [time]. The recipient has not received it. Please confirm the last known location, the next action and when I should expect another update.

Use the provider's support channel for full addresses and personal contact details. A public complaint should not expose the recipient's phone number or sensitive documents.

## If tracking says delivered

Ask the recipient to check with authorised reception staff, household members or anyone allowed to accept the parcel. If nobody received it, report the mismatch promptly and request the delivery record.

Avoid accusing a named individual before the facts are established. Keep screenshots, order messages and booking records for the investigation.

## If delivery cannot be completed

Ask whether the available options are another attempt, collection or return. Confirm any charges and who must approve the change before arranging it.

For a valuable parcel, ask about the claim process and reporting deadline early. Do not assume that a late delivery automatically qualifies for compensation or a refund. [Gosendeet's terms](https://gosendeet.com/terms) make liability and insurance conditions carrier-dependent.

## What should I tell my customer?

Share the confirmed status, what you have requested and when you will update them again. Do not invent a new arrival time to reassure them.
