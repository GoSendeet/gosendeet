# Waybill or Doorstep Delivery: Which Should I Choose for Interstate Shipping?

By Ore

You ask about interstate delivery and one person says “just waybill it,” while another recommends a courier to the receiver's door. Which is more practical depends on who will handle the parcel at each end.

**Compare the actual handover method and complete cost, not just the label.** In everyday usage, “waybill” often means sending goods through a transport or terminal service. A waybill is also a shipping document, so the word alone does not establish how delivery works.

## Ask where the parcel will be handed over

Confirm the precise pickup, drop-off and destination collection locations. A bus operator, terminal-based courier and doorstep service may have different procedures even on the same route.

Do not assume that using a waybill means the parcel will reach the receiver's home. Equally, a courier booking may include terminal collection unless you selected home delivery.

[GIG Logistics' FAQ](https://giglogistics.com/mfaq/) explains that home delivery depends on listed locations. Confirm the selected provider's conditions for your exact address.

## Compare the work as well as the charge

| Question | Terminal collection | Doorstep delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Who takes the parcel to the origin point? | Sender or an arranged collector | Depends on whether pickup is included |
| Who receives it at destination? | Receiver or authorised collector | Named recipient or authorised receiver |
| Is another local journey needed? | Usually worth checking | Check address coverage and access |
| What if nobody is available? | Ask about holding and collection rules | Ask about another attempt or return |

Neither option removes the need for working contact numbers, clear item details and a booking record.

## Calculate the complete journey

Add the quoted transport charge, getting the parcel to the origin terminal, the receiver's journey to collect it and any required local delivery afterwards.

For illustration, a ₦4,000 terminal shipment plus ₦1,000 transport at each end totals ₦6,000. A ₦5,500 pickup-and-doorstep quote would cost less in that scenario. These numbers explain the arithmetic; they are not current route prices.

If both people live beside the terminals, the outcome could be different. Time and convenience also count, particularly for someone who would need to leave work to collect.

## Consider what you are sending

For a valuable, fragile or time-sensitive item, ask about acceptance, handling, tracking and liability before choosing either method. A familiar bus route is not a substitute for written parcel conditions.

Ask what evidence is issued at handover and how the provider records delivery or collection. If another person will collect, confirm authorisation and identification requirements in advance.

## Do not confuse departure time with arrival availability

A vehicle arriving in the destination city does not necessarily mean a parcel is immediately ready for collection. Ask when and how the receiver will be notified.

For doorstep service, check the estimated window for the final delivery leg. Sorting and local distribution are part of the journey.
