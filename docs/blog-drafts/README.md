## Editorial and source notes

- Current-event claims link to their sources in the article body. The Uber Send
  article should be checked again at publication because it concerns a changing
  market. Competitor mentions are not a tested performance ranking.
- Pricing examples are labelled hypothetical. No invented live quotes or
  nationwide price tables are presented as market rates.
- Product claims were checked against the repository's current home-page,
  delivery-booking, tracking and terms content. The live homepage could not be
  retrieved by the research tool. Direct coverage is described conservatively
  as currently focused on Lagos and Ibadan; other courier options require a
  route-level quote. Confirm this wording with current operations before release.
- No claims are made about guaranteed arrival slots, universal electronics
  acceptance, full-value insurance, stock management, recurring collections,
  bulk automation or buyer protection.
- Article 5 focuses on clothing fulfilment; article 11 on general delivery admin;
  article 15 on shipping clothing to different destinations. Articles 3, 6 and 9
  cover quote assessment, buyer affordability and choosing a cheaper method.
  Keep those distinctions when editing to avoid near-duplicate published pages.
- Relative article links work within this folder. Convert them to the final
  public URLs when integrating the Markdown into the site.
- Keyword phrases reflect editorial targeting and observed research themes;
  they are not measured search-volume figures or promises of search rankings.
